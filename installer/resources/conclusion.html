You're all set

Three steps and the mic takes over.

Get cutting

  1. Open Premiere Pro.
  2. Go to Window → Extensions → Arrow Switch.
  3. Add your cameras and mics, hit SYNC & BUILD, then LISTEN! and CUT IT!

Good to know

  • ⌘ Enter does the next step, whatever it happens to be.
  • The mic is rude by default. The face button, top right, makes it nice.

To remove it later, run Uninstall Arrow Switch from the disk image.
