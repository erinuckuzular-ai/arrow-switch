You're all set

Three steps and the mic takes over.

Get cutting

  1. 1Open Premiere Pro.
  2. 2Go to Window → Extensions → Arrow Switch.
  3. 3Drop in your cameras and mics, hit SYNC & BUILD, then LISTEN! and CUT IT!

Good to know

  • ⌘⌘ Enter does the next step, whatever it is.
  • 🙂The mic is rude by default. The face button up top makes it nice.

To remove it later, run Uninstall Arrow Switch from the disk image.
