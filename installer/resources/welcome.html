Arrow Switch

Automatic multicam podcast editing for Adobe Premiere Pro.

Before you start

  • ⏻Quit Premiere Pro. It only sees new panels when it starts up.
  • ✓Premiere Pro 2022 or newer, on macOS.

What gets installed

  • 🎛The Arrow Switch panel, under Window → Extensions.
  • 🎧A bundled audio decoder, so nothing else to install.

Your projects are never changed: every cut lands on a new sequence.
